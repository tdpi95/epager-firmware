# M5: Real BLE, custom service and ANCS client (2026-10-14)

Approved 2026-10-14 with: both phone families to test with, the Current Time Service client later, a fourth bond
replaces the oldest. **Everything below builds and is host-tested; on the board only the boot with BLE advertising has been seen** (see
"Not verified"); no phone has connected.

## Done

- **Storage:** `kv_storage_t` on NVS (`components/app/src/app_kv.c`, D69); config, seen apps and the transfer
  journal survive a reset (RAM fallback if NVS cannot open).
- **`components/ble_core`** (D67): GATT service from `gatt.json` (Capabilities readable before pairing; every other
  characteristic and Battery need an encrypted, authenticated link, 128-bit key), Device Information, advertising per
  D7 with Apple's intervals, Secure Connections passkey pairing (DisplayOnly), new bonds only in pairing mode,
  three bonds with the oldest replaced, a connection that does not secure the link is dropped after 20 s, connection
  parameters by Apple's rules, MTU 517, FIFOs between the NimBLE host task and the app task. Portable `ble_util.c`
  (advertising bytes, name, passkey, intervals, parameters, policy, FIFO) with `test_ble`.
- **`components/ancs_client`** (D68): NimBLE discovery, subscription, retry and Service Changed (`ancs_glue.c`);
  portable `ancs_sm.c` (one request at a time, queue, name cache, removals, timeouts, negative action) with
  `test_ancs` (19 tests incl. a 100,000-step fuzz run), 95 % covered. `core_params_t.on_dismissed` sends the negative
  action when a card of an iPhone notification is dismissed on the board.
- **UI:** the passkey on the colour pairing screen and in place of the card on the Paper, golden images
  `pairing_passkey` (both boards), fake board `passkey` command.
- **Tools:** `tools/test_client --ble ADDRESS [--passkey N] [--no-pair]` pairs through a BlueZ agent
  (`bluez_agent.py`, registers with BlueZ; never run against a board); `bench` works without the asset sink.
- **Independent review** of the glue against the NimBLE sources (a subagent, read-only). Verified: `sec_state.bonded` is
  set before `ENC_CHANGE`, bonded private addresses resolve, the thread-safety of the calls used from the app task.
  Found and fixed: a "just works" peer leaves a persisted bond outside pairing mode that could push real phones out
  (the bond is now deleted, `BT_NIMBLE_SM_LVL=3`); `MAX_CCCDS` 8 would make the second phone unpair the first (24);
  4 KiB host task stack (6 KiB, static buffers); control events can be dropped by a full FIFO (reserve);
  failed connection-parameter requests were never retried; `on_reset` left the glue stale; ANCS retries stopped after
  seven minutes, Service Changed was watched only when ANCS was missing, a race on the Control Point handle, a late
  write result mistaken for the next one; the loop slept 1 s with the screen off while notifications waited.
- **Docs:** PLAN D67-D69, risks, §9; hw-checklist 39-53; AGENTS.md; `tools/README.md`.

## Verified, and how

- Host: `ctest` 14/14 under ASan+UBSan; `pytest tools` 282 passed (golden images: only the two new ones added);
  coverage 98.1 % (`ble_util.c` 100 %, `ancs_sm.c` 95.5 %); `contract_check.py` OK (no contract change);
  `feature_check.sh`, `lvgl_conf_check.py`, `xtensa_check.sh` (both new portable files, `-Werror`) green.
- Firmware: both variants build from clean with zero warnings. Paper 868,624 B (slot 3 MiB, 3.6x), LCD 2.83 MB
  with the debug console and BLE stats (slot 4 MiB, 1.48x; the product build is about 2.79 MB, 1.5x). The effective
  sdkconfig has SC-only, level 3, 24 CCCDs, the central role (needed for NimBLE's GATT client), 1 connection, 3 bonds.

## Not verified

- **Everything on the radio and on a phone** (checklist 39-53): pairing with the passkey on Android and iOS, Secure
  Connections only, the weak-bond deletion, bonded reconnects (private addresses), the connection parameters each phone
  grants, MTU, throughput, ANCS consent and Service Changed on iOS, the replay, the negative action, advertising
  discovery times, light sleep with a link, content-free logs. The BlueZ agent, and `test_client --ble` against a board.
- **The flash** went through on the fourth attempt (2026-10-14, `/dev/ttyACM0`, `build/lcd-ble`: product code plus
  the debug console and `CONFIG_EPAGER_BLE_STATS`): the first two could not connect (the board gave no console output and
  no download mode), the third used a port that had moved to `/dev/ttyACM1` and was stopped by the tool's permission
  check, the fourth worked after the user's go-ahead. Boot log: `ble ok`, advertising as `epager-F236` at 20 ms, no bonds;
  then, with one Android phone and nRF Connect (2026-10-14): passkey pairing and bonding, the GATT table, Capabilities decoded, the 15 s fast then relaxed connection parameters, the ANCS probe backing off on Android, and a PING answered through the encrypted link with the screen off (checklist 39-41, 49, 50, 52 partly). Not run: iOS and ANCS, the Android app, the laptop client, reconnect of a bonded phone, bond limits, the unsecured-link timeout.
- Pairing mode has no timeout (first boot leaves it on until a phone bonds or the user leaves it); the Status
  characteristic can read empty for a moment after the link becomes secure; the rx FIFO copies from PSRAM under a spinlock.
