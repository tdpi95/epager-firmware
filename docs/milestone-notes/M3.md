## Update: app icons (2026-10-11, after contract 0.4.0)

M3 was extended to show per-app notification icons (contract 0.4.0, PLAN D34/D35). The numbers in
the sections above are from before this update; the ones below replace them.

- **Done:**
  - `ui_common`: the placeholder card shows a 24x24 icon left of the title (the title wraps beside it and
    the body starts under the taller of the two). `ui_params_t.blob` gives the UI the board's blob storage;
    on each render it loads the installed `app_icon` for `xfer_app_icon_id(app_id)` (loader re-checks the
    container) or draws the built-in icon of the category. `ui_assets_changed()` re-reads icons after an
    install or delete; `ui_current_icon()` reports builtin/app for tests.
  - `xfer` gets an `on_asset` callback (after install and delete) and `xfer_asset_blob_name()`; `core`
    forwards `on_asset`. No contract change, no wire change.
  - `tools/assets/icon_gen.py`: the 12 built-in category icons (bell, handset with arrow, handset with
    cross, voicemail reels, speech bubble, calendar, envelope, newspaper, heart, bar chart, map pin, play
    button) drawn with Pillow and committed as `components/ui_common/src/ui_icons.c`; `--check`, `--preview`.
    Looked at the contact sheet and all golden images.
  - Fake board / tools: `fake_board/loopback.py` (synchronous in-process client: installs and deletes
    assets through the real protocol path), `sample_icons.py`, console `icon` / `icon-remove`, scene `icons`,
    test client flows `app_icons` (any board, safe on hardware) and `app_icons_screen` (pixel checks on the
    simulated panel), `tools/tests/test_icons.py`.
  - Golden images: 3 new (`icon_builtin`, `icon_app`, `icon_other_app_builtin`) and 9 regenerated (every
    scene with a card now has an icon, and the title starts at x = 32 instead of 4).
- **Verified (how):**
  - `ctest --test-dir build/host` (ASan+UBSan): 8/8; `test_ui` is now 16 tests, the new ones compare the
    24x24 icon region pixel for pixel: every category's built-in icon (and an out-of-range value), the
    installed app icon, a case-different app ID (not matched), an icon installed, replaced and deleted
    while the card is shown (one extra partial refresh, none for a non-icon asset), malformed files (wrong
    asset type, format, size, length) ignored, no blob storage, and a long title kept on one line beside the icon.
  - `pytest tools`: **230 passed** (was 214): both new flows over real TCP, in one session with all other
    flows, the console commands, loopback installs including the board's `IMAGE_INVALID`, the built-in icon

  - Three deliberate mutations failed the tests: the fake board not forwarding `on_asset`
    (`app_icons_screen`), the icon bits not inverted (4 `test_ui` failures), the app icon lookup disabled
    (2). Restored afterwards.
  - Portable-core coverage 98.1 %; `contract_check.py` OK; `xtensa_check.sh` warning-free (core 3.6 KB);
    `font_gen.py --check` and `icon_gen.py --check` clean.
